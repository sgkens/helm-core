[h1]HELM Toolbox and layouts[/h1]

HELM Toolbox is the visual companion to the documentation. It runs locally in the browser and uses the real HELM parser and renderer compiled to WebAssembly.

[list]
[*]Choose a shipped starter layout.
[*]Insert blocks, inventory items, components and built-in templates from the palette.
[*]Use the inspector for colors, titles, positions, panels and component options.
[*]Drag the editor divider to give Custom Data more room.
[*]Switch hardware profiles to check wide, square and cockpit surfaces.
[*]Pause or resume simulated animation while inspecting a frame.
[/list]

Inserted items and components add missing @bind aliases automatically. Review generated block/group names before copying the result into the game.

[h2]Responsive layouts[/h2]

Prefer [code]profile: auto[/code] for a layout intended for several surface sizes. Use grids and panels to group related telemetry; keep critical labels short enough for narrow cockpit screens. SVG previews scale cleanly, but the in-game font atlas and surface viewport remain the final authority.

[h2]Presets and templates[/h2]

Presets are complete layout files. Built-in templates are reusable DSL fragments invoked with `@use`. Start from a preset when designing a whole console and use a template when repeating a subsystem with different block-group arguments.
