[h1]HELM Core[/h1]

HELM (High-Efficiency Lightweight Monitor) turns Space Engineers LCDs and cockpit surfaces into configurable telemetry displays. A compact text DSL controls screens, panels, gauges, colors and block bindings. HELM Core runs in a vanilla Programmable Block; no client mod is required.

[h2]Install from the Workshop[/h2]

[olist]
[*]Subscribe to HELM Core on the Steam Workshop.
[*]Place a Programmable Block and the LCDs or cockpit surfaces you want to use.
[*]Open the Programmable Block, choose Edit, Browse Scripts, select HELM Core, and run it.
[*]Paste a layout into the Programmable Block's Custom Data.
[*]Make the block or group names in each @bind line match your grid.
[/olist]

HELM writes only to surfaces declared by the active layout. Back up existing Custom Data before replacing it.
